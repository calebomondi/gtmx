# List hygiene: protect the founder's inbox

Emails come from the founder's own mailbox. Bad addresses and unwanted emails
hurt its reputation, and then even good emails land in spam. Keep the list
clean before anything is drafted.

## Before drafting
- **Only verified addresses.** A contact's email must have passed the check
  (its domain receives mail). Skip guessed or unchecked addresses, and tell the
  founder whose address is missing rather than invent one.
- **Keep bounces under 2%.** If more than 1 in 50 recent emails bounced, stop
  drafting new first emails and tell the founder the list needs checking.
- **One person per company at a time** for a first touch, unless the founder
  asks otherwise. A second person only after the first has had their follow-ups.
- **Check the CRM first** when one is connected: never cold-email an existing
  customer, an open deal, or someone the CRM says not to contact.

## After a bounce
- Mark that address as bad and never draft to it again.
- Look for another person at the company, and tell the founder.

## Always respect
- **Unsubscribe and "not interested"**: do-not-contact, for good.
- **Role addresses** (info@, hello@): only when no named person can be found,
  and say so.
- **The founder's own rules** (standing instructions) about who not to contact.
